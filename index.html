Ebru'sFirst Exhibition

Art textiles, drawings and colour studies by Ebru.

Opening hours: after sunset only. The artist is allergic to the sun. Entry: one bear hug. Guests: one artist, one Dracula.

A note from the curator

On 18 September I told you I wanted to make an “Ebru Art Gallery” for you. You said: no hurry, your own work comes first.

So I waited, and I kept every picture you sent me. Here they are, on a wall, with a light on each one.

Tap a work to see it bigger. Hold your finger on it to look closer. Most works have no name yet. You are the artist, so you can name them.

Alex, your favourite student
