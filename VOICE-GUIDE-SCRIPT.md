# Voice guide script

Five short recordings, about 30 to 45 seconds each. The same words appear on the page under "Read along", in English and Turkish, so she can read while she listens.

**How to record**
- Speak slowly, about half your normal speed. Your songs pause by themselves while your voice plays, so record without music behind you. Leave a small pause at each "/".
- Use a quiet room. Hold the phone about a hand's width from your mouth.
- iPhone Voice Memos or Android Recorder are fine. Save as .m4a (or .mp3).
- Name the files exactly: lobby.m4a, room-1.m4a, room-2.m4a, room-3.m4a, exit.m4a, and put them in the `audio` folder.

---

## 1. Lobby — lobby.m4a

Hoş geldin, aşkım. / Welcome to your first exhibition. / I am Alex, your guide tonight. / Don't worry, it is after sunset, so Dracula is safe. / Take your time and walk slowly. / Tap any work to see it bigger, / and hold your finger on it to look closer. / When you are ready, go to Room One.

## 2. Room 1, Art Textiles — room-1.m4a

This is Room One: art textiles. / The red one is the work everyone loves. / But you know me. I love all of them. / The blue and gold one was your profile picture. / On the first night, I did not even look at it. / Now I cannot stop looking. / Most of these works have no name yet. / You are the artist. Please give them names.

## 3. Room 2, Drawings — room-2.m4a

Room Two: drawings. Karakalem. / You told me you made one hundred drawings a week, for one class, / and your fingers hurt. / I can see all those hours here. / The leopard is ready to jump. / And the glass bottle taught me a new word: negative drawing. / Thank you, öğretmenim.

## 4. Room 3, Colour Studies — room-3.m4a

Room Three: colour studies. / Five small questions about colour. / Red inside red. / A shell in red and blue. / A yellow mosaic, like sunlight, but safe for my Dracula. / Now look at the violet spiral for ten seconds. / Does it turn? / Or do you turn?

## 5. Exit — exit.m4a

Thank you for visiting, my artist. / Before you go, here are the songs I made for you, / and a little box where you can write me a note. / And somewhere in these rooms, a bear and a kitten are hiding. / Can you find them all? / İyi geceler, bitanem.

---

If you change any words when you record, change the matching text in `index.html` too (search for `var GUIDE`), so the read-along text matches your voice.
